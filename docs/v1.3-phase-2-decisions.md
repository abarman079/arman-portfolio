# V1.3 Phase 2 decisions

## Lower-homepage hierarchy

- Capabilities is now a compact evidence register. It links four verified capability areas to inspectable projects without repeating screenshots or technology stacks.
- The Capability R3F scene was removed. Its abstract spatial forms did not communicate additional project evidence; the Interactive Lab remains the single lower-page WebGL enhancement.
- The archive is a quieter project registry: three featured rows with real media, followed by five compact supporting rows. Technology lists and repeated architecture facts were removed from the homepage treatment.
- About now contains one owner-approved working statement, confirmed location, and a resume path. Repeated project imagery, technology groups, project counts, and profile-link clusters were removed.
- Contact retains the direct email path and validated form behavior. Decorative system tokens, pseudo-routing labels, pointer physics, repeated location, and duplicate social links were removed.
- The footer is a compact navigation/copyright close rather than another oversized identity scene.

## Interactive Lab clarification

The existing custom GLSL shader is retained as a graphics-programming demonstration. Public copy now describes its actual operations—procedural pattern, distortion/edge emphasis, and color threshold—and explicitly states that it is not a trained model or an ML prediction. Abstract labels such as “Signal field” and “Threshold field” were removed. Mobile and reduced-motion modes keep the designed static transformation sequence and do not instantiate WebGL.

## Motion and lifecycle

- Homepage motion remains scoped to the existing GSAP/ScrollTrigger runtime; no dependency was added.
- Capability-card, archive-scan, About-mosaic, Contact-token, and Contact-pointer animation systems were removed.
- Lab progress keeps one scrubbed timeline, no pin, and the existing visibility/document-hidden lifecycle.
- The flagship story and project-route continuity systems remain unchanged except for normal layout compatibility.
- Content remains visible without enhancement, and reduced motion disables smooth scrolling, flagship choreography, Lab WebGL, and large reveals.
